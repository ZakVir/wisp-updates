# Wisp

Wisp is a push-to-talk helper for your Mac. Hold **Control + Option**, ask anything out loud, and let go. Wisp
answers in a natural voice with captions. When your question is about something on your screen, a small cursor
flies over to show you the right button.

This repository only hosts Wisp's downloads and its update feed. Wisp keeps itself up to date from here.

## Install

1. Download the latest **Wisp-x.y.z.dmg** from [Releases](../../releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag **Wisp** into **Applications**.
3. Open Wisp from Applications. The first time, macOS may say it can't check Wisp for malicious software. If it does:
   1. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**.
   2. Scroll down to the message about Wisp and click **Open Anyway**.
   3. Confirm.
4. Follow the short welcome guide:
   - it downloads Wisp's on-device speech (about 260 MB);
   - you connect a free NVIDIA account for the AI;
   - you allow the microphone, the shortcut and screen access.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer).

## Privacy

- Your voice is turned into text on your Mac, and answers are spoken on your Mac.
- While you hold the shortcut, Wisp takes a screenshot of your screen. That screenshot and your question go to the AI
  service you connected, and nothing is kept.
- Wisp has no analytics.
